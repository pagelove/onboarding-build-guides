## What to build
Contact records, conversation history, follow-up dates, search and a clear contact detail view.

Build a working responsive result with useful empty/loading/error states and accessible controls. Keep it focused and editable.

## Personalization, only when enabled
Use my business name as the workspace/account name and my known role, industry, products/services and customer types to tailor the contact fields, relationship labels and follow-up workflow. Prefill only contacts, organizations, dated conversations, commitments and follow-up dates I have actually shared and want included. Distinguish my own business from employers and clients; leave unknown contacts and interactions empty.
